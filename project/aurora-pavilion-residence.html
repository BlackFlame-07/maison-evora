Aurora Pavilion Residence

Al Marmoom Desert Reserve, Dubai, UAE

Infomation

Set within the vast golden dunes of the Al Marmoom Desert Reserve, Aurora Pavilion Residence is an architectural and interior design project that reinterprets desert living through a contemporary lens, blending sculptural minimalism, earth-toned materials, and seamless spatial transitions that dissolve the boundary between built form and the expansive desert horizon.

Scope Of Work

Creating a cohesive living environment where architecture and interior design merge into a serene experiential journey, connecting inhabitants with the silence, light, and vastness of the desert landscape.

Architectural Design

The design team develops low-lying, wind-responsive volumes that emerge organically from the dunes, organizing spaces around natural light, thermal comfort, and framed desert vistas, while preserving a continuous dialogue with the surrounding arid environment.

Interior Design

The material palette features rammed earth plaster, sandblasted limestone, dark oak accents, woven desert textiles, and matte mineral finishes, establishing a calm, tactile atmosphere rooted in contemporary desert aesthetics.

Custom Furniture

All interior furnishings are custom-designed to reflect the architectural language, emphasizing sculptural simplicity, ergonomic comfort, and a seamless integration with spatial proportions and desert-inspired tones.

Landscape & Outdoor Living

Sunken courtyards, shaded pergolas, reflective pools, and native xerophytic planting are carefully integrated across the site, extending living spaces outward and enhancing the immersive connection between architecture and desert nature.

Exhibition Space

A cascading crystal installation composed of frosted, cognac, and clear elements, drawing the eye upward and accentuating the apartment’s impressive ceiling heights.
